# Buffered Studio-Grade Contact Microphone

This is an open-source, buffered, studio-grade contact microphone designed for professional audio applications. Unlike traditional piezo contact mics, this mic features an onboard buffer circuit that eliminates impedance mismatch issues, ensuring a full-spectrum frequency response without the common high-pass filtering effect. It connects via a balanced XLR output and runs on phantom power, making it compatible with a wide range of professional audio equipment.

## Features
- **Buffered Circuit**: Eliminates impedance mismatch, allowing for a full-range frequency response.
- **XLR Balanced Output**: Ensures low-noise and interference-free operation.
- **Phantom Powered**: No need for external batteries or power supplies.
- **Piezo Disc Capsule**: Captures subtle vibrations with extreme sensitivity.
- **Studio-Grade Sensitivity**: Capable of detecting even the most delicate surface vibrations.

## Benefits Over Traditional Contact Mics
- **No High-Pass Effect**: Unlike standard piezo microphones, this design prevents the loss of low frequencies.
- **Greater Sensitivity**: Captures a broader spectrum of sound with high fidelity.
- **Professional Connectivity**: The XLR output ensures seamless integration with studio and stage equipment.

## Technical Specifications
- **Microphone Capsule**: Piezo Disc
- **Output**: Balanced XLR
- **Power**: Phantom Power (48V)
- **Frequency Response**: Full-range (avoids typical high-pass filtering of piezo mics)
- **Impedance Matching**: Built-in active buffer circuit

## Installation & Usage
1. Connect the microphone to an XLR input on an audio interface, mixer, or recorder with phantom power enabled.
2. Securely attach the piezo disc to the desired surface using tape, putty, or a contact adhesive for optimal vibration transmission.
3. Adjust input gain on your recording device to achieve the desired signal level.

## Availability
The microphone can be purchased preassembled and soldered, or users can download a `.rar` file containing all the Gerber files needed for production and assemble it themselves.

## Applications
- **Musical Instruments**: Capture vibrations from guitars, violins, pianos, and other acoustic instruments.
- **Sound Design & Foley**: Record subtle surface vibrations for experimental audio.
- **Scientific & Industrial Use**: Monitor mechanical vibrations and structural resonance.
